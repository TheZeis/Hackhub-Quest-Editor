> **Correction banner (r250/r251).** Several rows below — r237 to r249 in
> particular — were written while the agent working those rounds was
> hallucinating, and they state conclusions that have since been disproved.
> They are archived here **as written**, because the record of what was
> believed is part of the evidence. Before acting on any row in the r244–r249
> range, read [`../plans/r250-r237-r249-audit.md`](../plans/r250-r237-r249-audit.md).
> The two headline corrections:
>
> - A mail's `to:` field is **not** what loses it. A `to:` naming a mailbox
>   that does not exist is accepted, given an id, and silently dropped; a `to:`
>   filled from `Mail.getPlayerEmail()` delivers, and our r211 run proves it in
>   game.
> - The "page context is refused" conclusion (r245) is withdrawn. Its A/B
>   changed two variables at once, and the probe's own bridge test — a send
>   from trusted context carrying the same fabricated address — failed too.
